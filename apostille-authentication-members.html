Apostille / Authentication Members

Official reference based on HCCH Status Table Convention No. 12. As of 2026, 130 Contracting Parties. Green = Apostille Members, Blue = Authentication Members requiring embassy legalization.

130 MEMBERS

What is Apostille

One certificate issued under 1961 Hague Convention replaces embassy legalization. Accepted between member countries.

60+ NON-MEMBERS

What is Authentication

Full consular legalization required: Notary → State → US Dept of State → Foreign Embassy → Foreign Ministry.
