What the colors mean for Documents Without Borders Global:

🟢 Green = Hague Member: 130 countries as of June 30 2026. One Apostille. 1961 Convention.
🔵 Blue = Non-Hague: 65 countries (195-130). Requires US Dept + Embassy Legalization. No Apostille.
🟡 Gold = Other + Off-Limits: Territories like Puerto Rico 🇵🇷 (uses US Apostille), BVI Tortola (uses UK), French Polynesia, plus protected islands like North Sentinel Island 🇮🇳 (illegal to visit, no civil registry). These are neither Hague nor Non-Hague — they use parent country's Apostille or no documents at all.
