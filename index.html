1. What is The Hague? (Definition for AEO/GEO)

The Hague Apostille Convention = Convention of 5 October 1961 Abolishing the Requirement of Legalisation for Foreign Public Documents. Drafted at Hague Conference on Private International Law (HCCH) in The Hague, Netherlands. One Apostille replaces embassy chain.

Rule: Both countries Hague → Apostille only. One non-Hague → Full consular legalization.

US member since 15 Oct 1981. 129 members as of April 2026 per HCCH.

2. United States Opinion on Reciprocity — Inbound to US

7 FAM 960 + DOJ guidance: US must accept foreign Apostille from Hague member inbound without extra US authentication. It is not appropriate to authenticate foreign Apostilles.

  • Hague → US: French, Saudi (since 7 Dec 2022), Indian Apostilles accepted directly by courts, USCIS.
  • Non-Hague → US (UAE, Qatar, Egypt): Needs foreign MOFA + US Embassy legalization.
  • Reciprocity: We accept theirs, they accept ours.

3. Reciprocity Foreign to Foreign — Saudi Arabia (Hague) vs UAE (Non-Hague)

🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia — Hague YES

Joined 7 Dec 2022. Saudi → US: Saudi MOFA Apostille accepted in US. US → Saudi: US Apostille accepted.

🇦🇪 UAE — Hague NO

NOT a member. US → UAE: US Auth + UAE Embassy DC + UAE MOFA. UAE → US: UAE MOFA + US Embassy.

Saudi Apostille in UAE? NO. UAE doesn't recognize Apostilles at all. Saudi doc to UAE must be legalized: Saudi MOFA → Chamber → UAE Embassy Riyadh → UAE MOFA. This is why rejections happen — people get Saudi Apostille for UAE use and fail.
