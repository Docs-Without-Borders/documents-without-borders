We don't just notarize documents. We unify the people who do.

Documents Without Borders is a global initiative to meet and greet with notaries around the globe, to create a unity of professionals in the notary space — globally.

All persons that would be affiliated to the notary space are welcome: Notaries Public, Mobile Notaries, RON Notaries, Notario, Signing Agents, Apostille Agents, Translators, Consular Professionals, Attorneys, Title Officers, Court Clerks, and Document Specialists.

Instead of calling it the unification of global like-minded notaries, we call it...

NOTARIZATIONSHIP

What is Notarizationship?

Coined by Oswald Joseph Bien-Aimé, CEO & Chief Notary Officer, Notarizationship is the state of being united as global notary-minded professionals. It's fellowship. It's fraternity. It's a professional citizenship without borders.

Notary + Citizenship + Relationship = Notarizationship

  • Meet & Greet: City chapters, virtual cafés
  • Unify: One code, one directory, one voice
  • Uplift: Mentorship, education, advocacy

Our Manifesto

1. Meet

We exist to meet. No notary should work in isolation. From New York to Port-au-Prince to Paris to Dubai — we show up for each other.

2. Greet

We greet with respect. Every culture, language, seal, and signature is honored. Kreyòl, English, Arabic, Spanish — all languages of trust.

3. Unify

We unify. Fragmented associations divide us. Notarizationship connects Notaries Public, Notarios, Mobile Notaries, RON, Translators, and all affiliated professions under one global roof.

Who Belongs in Notarizationship?

If your work touches a seal, a signature, a stamp, a legalization — you belong.

Core Notary
Notaries Public, Mobile Notaries, RON / IPEN Notaries, Notario Público, Signing Agents
Affiliated Space
Translators, Apostille Agents, Consular Liaisons, Title & Escrow, Court Clerks, Attorneys
Global Allies
Document Couriers, Legalization Services, Language Justice Advocates, Legal Tech Builders
