What is The Hague Apostille Convention

The Convention of 5 October 1961 Abolishing the Requirement of Legalisation for Foreign Public Documents simplifies document authentication between member countries through a single Apostille certificate.

The United States has been a member since 15 October 1981. As of 2026, 129 countries are members.

Understanding Reciprocity

United States

Documents from Hague members with an Apostille are accepted in the United States without additional authentication. Documents from non-members require consular legalization.

Saudi Arabia and United Arab Emirates

Saudi Arabia is a Hague member since December 2022. The United Arab Emirates is not a member. An Apostille issued by a Hague member is not accepted in the UAE, which requires embassy legalization.
